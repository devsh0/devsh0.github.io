Hi, I am Devashish. I like computers. With this blog I aim to share what I know about them.

I don't use AI to generate or even check any of what I post here (except, maybe in the future,
diagrams that are hard to draw and aren't easily available on the Internet). So you may find subtle
(sometimes not so subtle) grammatical or factual errors. I'd appreciate if you point them out in
the comment section, but please make sure you don't post AI generated stuff in the comment section.
AI, LLMs etc. are useful tools, but I do believe that some things should remain human.

Have fun!
